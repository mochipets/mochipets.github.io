Focus

What is Strict Mode, and when does a session fail?
  • If you leave the app for too long during a focus session, the session fails and gives no rewards. The default limit is 25 seconds; change it in Focus › Settings › Pomodoro Flow & Rules.
  • Locking your phone or taking a call doesn't count as leaving.
  • You get 2 Strict Passes every week. If you're away too long, one is used automatically and your session keeps going (you can turn off Auto-Use Strict Passes).
  • Don't want this rule? Turn off Strict Mode in Settings on the Focus tab.
I want to use a dictionary or calculator without it counting as leaving

Use Block Apps While Focusing instead: only the distracting apps you choose are blocked, other apps can be used freely, and it won't count as a Strict Mode failure. Set it up in Focus › Settings › Block Distracting Apps; it needs your Face ID or passcode to allow Screen Time.

I didn't get a notification when my focus session ended

In the iPhone Settings app, go to Notifications › Mochi Pets and make sure notifications are on, and check that a Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them. In the app, Settings › Notifications lets you turn each kind of reminder on or off.

Steps and hatching

My steps aren't updating and I can't get tickets
  1. Open the Health app › your profile picture (top right) › Apps › Mochi Pets, and make sure Steps is allowed.
  2. Go back to Mochi Pets and open the Hatch tab; your steps update automatically.
  3. Every 2,000 steps gives 1 ticket, up to 5 a day. Steps entered by hand don't count.

Mochi Pets Plus and payments

I bought Plus, but it's gone after reinstalling or switching phones

In the app, go to Settings › Mochi Pets Plus and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy it.

Will the free trial charge me automatically? How do I cancel?

Monthly and yearly plans include a 7-day free trial, and the app reminds you the day before it ends. To stop it from renewing, cancel in the iPhone Settings app › your Apple Account › Subscriptions. Canceling during the trial means you won't be charged.

How do I cancel a subscription or get a refund?
  • Cancel: iPhone Settings › your Apple Account › Subscriptions, or tap Manage Subscription on the Plus page in the app. You keep Plus until the end of the period.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
Can I play without Plus?

Yes. Focusing, caring for pets, hatching, seasonal events, Exam Sprint and adventure stories are all free. Plus adds extra looks and advanced stats; it never makes pets stronger or changes hatching odds.

Data and new phones

How do I move my pets to a new phone?
  1. On your old phone, go to Settings › Backup › Export Backup and save the file to Files or iCloud Drive.
  2. Install Mochi Pets on your new phone and choose the file in Settings › Backup › Restore from Backup.
  3. If iCloud Sync is on, just sign in with the same Apple Account and your data comes back automatically.
Is my data uploaded?

No. Your data stays on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud when iCloud Sync is on; we can't see it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete all my data?

Deleting the app deletes its data on your device. Data in iCloud can be deleted in iPhone Settings › your Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Account Storage. This can't be undone, so consider exporting a backup first.
